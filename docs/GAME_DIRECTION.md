# Game Direction

## One-Line Pitch

A dark top-down/2.5D action strategy game where a strong, readable protagonist pushes light into hostile space while the room fights back.

## Non-Negotiables

- Not a generic hero.
- Not a side-scrolling belt action game.
- Not a fixed countdown timer game.
- Not a passive errand/relic-management fantasy.
- Not copied from existing games.

## Player Fantasy

The player is not a chosen village boy. They are a competent, dangerous person entering a place that does not want them there.

The fantasy is:

- Step into a hostile room.
- Read danger instantly.
- Create safety.
- Strike hard.
- Keep several problems from collapsing at once.
- Choose what to abandon.

## Core Verbs

- Move.
- Strike.
- Carry light.
- Place/ignite/repair a ward.
- Pull civilians or allies into safety.
- Shut, brace, or break doors.
- Spend scarce fuel, charge, or stamina.

## First 30-Second Play Shape

The first prototype should prove this:

> A strong protagonist enters a collapsing ritual room and stabilizes it through violent action and smart handling, without a countdown timer.

Sequence:

1. The player enters a dark chapel or ward-room. A central safe light is active. Outside it, fog and silhouettes press inward.
2. The protagonist can kill weak enemies quickly with a large readable arc.
3. Two rifts open at the room edge. They are visible as pressure sources, not timer UI.
4. The player can keep fighting, or carry a ritual material to a ward point.
5. Carrying material reduces attack/mobility, creating Overcooked/Unrailed-style handling pressure without making the protagonist feel weak.
6. Ignoring a rift makes the room state worse: more enemies, darker edges, weaker safe light, or cracked barricades.
7. Spending a powerful attack can break a surround but consumes ward fuel or charge.
8. The room ends with a reward choice that changes the next room: strike power, vision/safe light, ward handling, or noise control.

This preserves the user's desired chaos and tension while avoiding a fixed time limit.

## Pressure Without Countdown

The game should feel urgent without a visible clock.

Pressure sources:

- A dark zone slowly reclaims unlit space.
- Enemies hear loud attacks and path toward noise.
- Barricades crack under pressure.
- Fires spread or die depending on fuel and airflow.
- Wards overload if too many threats hit them.
- Carrying a person or light changes movement and attack options.

The player is not racing a timer. They are stabilizing a bad situation while choosing which risk to accept.

## First Protagonist Target

Silhouette at 96px:

- Tall, sharp, compact body shape.
- Dark coat or armor mass.
- One bright face/helmet/mask mark.
- One strong red or gold accent.
- One weapon or tool that creates a large attack/interact arc.

The character must read as capable before the lore is explained.

## First Screenshot Target

16:9, top-down or isometric:

- Broken chapel, warded bridge, or gatehouse interior.
- Safe warm light in the lower/middle screen.
- Dark exterior pressure at edges.
- One protagonist in the light boundary.
- One large threat silhouette or clear incoming group outside the light.
- One visible action effect: swing arc, ward flare, or light beam.
- Minimal UI: health, fuel/ward charge, carried target count only.
