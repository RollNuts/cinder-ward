## What Actually Sells On Screen

Dark alone is not enough. Strong games keep the screen readable with:

- One instantly readable protagonist mark: white mask/head, red cloak, sharp helmet, glowing weapon, or attack arc.
- A visible threat or pressure source in the same frame.
- Strong small-area color: red, gold, cyan, green, or white against large dark shapes.
- Dark area used as rule space, not wallpaper. Darkness hides danger, limits vision, or frames a safe zone.
- High information density without equal-weight detail everywhere.

## Weak Direction To Avoid

The passive "relic collector / errand worker" direction is weak because the screen verb becomes "pick up" or "manage." The benchmark set sells stronger verbs first:

- Strike.
- Burn.
- Escape.
- Reclaim.
- Survive.
- Push into danger.
- Hold a collapsing position.

The protagonist may be small, but the action mark must be larger than the body.

## Recommended Direction

Use a dark fantasy top-down/2.5D game built around safety, light, and pressure.

Do not use a fixed countdown timer. Tension comes from:

- Enemy pressure.
- Limited safe light.
- Deteriorating rooms, doors, barricades, fires, or wards.
- Noise attracting danger.
- Strong actions producing consequences.
- Hard choices between rescuing, repairing, fighting, and pushing forward.

This borrows the legibility and chaos of Unrailed!/Overcooked without copying their timer-driven structure.

## First 30 Seconds Target

The player starts in a ruined chapel or warded street. A warm safe circle surrounds a broken brazier. Outside the light, silhouettes move in fog.

The player can:

- Move.
- Strike with a readable arc.
- Carry or place a light source.
- Repair or ignite one ward point.
- Decide whether to expand safety or push into darkness.

Audio:

- Safe zone: low wind, ember, distant bell, sparse drone.
- Outside safety: noise layer, low footfalls, unstable stereo details.
- Combat: dry attack impact over restrained music.
- Danger escalation: layers enter because the situation worsens, not because a timer expires.
